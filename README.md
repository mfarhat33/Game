# Inkfront

An original colour-territory strategy game on a hex board. Everything lives in `index.html`; open it in a browser to play.

- Pick a pigment: your territory turns that colour and absorbs every connected tile of it.
- You can't pick a pigment any side is currently wearing.
- Wells give charges. Surge (2) moves twice, Burst (3) takes your whole rim.
- Slate tiles are walls. Most tiles when the board is settled wins.

Modes: Skirmish against 1–3 computer rivals (three skill levels, three board sizes), a seeded Daily board, and two-player Pass & play.

# Flipfire

An original real-time tile battle in `flipfire.html`; open it in a browser to play.

- The field is a 22 by 36 grid split between you and a rival. Pick a card, press on the board, drag to aim and let go.
- Cannon cards (14 kinds) build a gun anywhere on your own ground. A cannon has health and keeps firing until the rival shoots it down.
- Each cannon is drawn as its own machine. With a force selected, the Aim button turns it to face backwards when you place it on ground you hold in the rival's half.
- Troop cards (Runner, Squad, Brute, Sapper) walk up the field taking tiles; dragon cards (Drake, Frost wyrm, Elder dragon) fly over everything and burn a path.
- Effect cards (29: strikes, guards, boosts, tricks) happen once.
- You bring 5 forces (cannons, troops, dragons) and 6 effects, and hold 2 forces and 3 effects at a time. Energy fills over time; the side holding less ground gains it faster.
- Coins come from wins, stars, first clears, win streaks, a goal on every stage and a daily reward. They buy workshop upgrades and raise each card up to level 10.
- Five named rivals rotate by stage. Later stages add plated tiles and walls; every fifth stage is a boss stage.
- Cleared stages can be replayed, an unfinished match resumes after a reload, and there is an option to stripe the rival's tiles.
- Every force has a role (Anchor, Pusher, Siege, Killer, Support) and the Forces tab tells you what your deck is missing.
- A match lasts 2:30. Energy doubles for the last 45 seconds, and a result within 2% goes to 20 seconds of overtime. Hold more of the field when the clock runs out to win.
