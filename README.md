# Inkfront

An original colour-territory strategy game on a hex board. Everything lives in `index.html`; open it in a browser to play.

- Pick a pigment: your territory turns that colour and absorbs every connected tile of it.
- You can't pick a pigment any side is currently wearing.
- Wells give charges. Surge (2) moves twice, Burst (3) takes your whole rim.
- Slate tiles are walls. Most tiles when the board is settled wins.

Modes: Skirmish against 1–3 computer rivals (three skill levels, three board sizes), a seeded Daily board, and two-player Pass & play.

# Flipfire

An original real-time tile battle in `flipfire.html`; open it in a browser to play.

- Slide to aim an auto-firing cannon. Shots flip the first rival tile they reach.
- Five cannons unlock as you clear stages: Tapper, Fork, Lance, Mortar, Ricochet.
- Build a 6-card deck from 13 cards (turrets, strikes, defences, tricks). Energy fills over time; you hold three cards at once.
- Rivals gain plated tiles and their own cards on later stages.
- Scrap earned each stage buys workshop upgrades. Hold more of the field after 60 seconds to win.
