How Inkfront works

  • Pick a pigment. Your whole territory turns that colour and soaks up every touching tile of the same colour, and every tile chained to those.
  • No sharing. You can't pick a pigment any side is wearing right now. Tap a pigment below, tap a tile on the board, or press 1–6.
  • Wells are the tiles with a dark dot. Each one you soak up gives a charge, up to five.
  • Surge costs 2 charges and lets you move twice. Burst costs 3 and takes every free tile on your rim, whatever its colour.
  • Slate tiles are grey and can't be taken. Use them as walls.
  • Winning. When every free tile is taken or sealed off behind one side, the side holding the most tiles wins.
